California Small Group Requirements Quick Check

To establish a California small group health insurance plan, you generally need:

1 to 100 Eligible Employees: At least one non-owner W-2 employee is typically required.
Active California Business Presence: Valid Federal EIN and California DE-9C quarterly wage report (or payroll summary).
Employer Contribution: The employer typically contributes at least 50% of the employee-only premium for the base plan.
Special enrollment waivers available during Year-End Relaxed Participation periods.
