Side-by-Side Comparison

Medicare Advantage vs. Medicare Supplement (Medigap)

Both paths provide vital protection beyond Original Medicare (Parts A & B). Which option fits your lifestyle and budget?

Medicare Advantage (Part C)

All-In-One Convenience

Private health plans approved by Medicare (HMO or PPO). They bundle Part A (hospital), Part B (medical), and usually Part D (prescriptions) into one single member card.

  • Often $0 or low monthly plan premium beyond standard Part B.
  • Includes built-in prescription drug coverage (Part D) in most plans.
  • Added supplemental perks: Routine dental cleanings, vision exams, hearing aids, OTC allowances, and gym memberships (SilverSneakers).
  • Network restriction: You must use network doctors (HMO/PPO) and co-pays apply as you use services.
Medicare Supplement (Medigap - Plan G/N)

Total Doctor Freedom

Supplemental policies that pay the “gaps” in Original Medicare (copayments, coinsurance, and deductibles).

  • See any doctor in the USA who accepts Medicare—no network constraints, no referrals required.
  • Predictable costs: With Plan G, after meeting a tiny annual Part B deductible, 100% of Medicare-covered medical bills are covered.
  • Guaranteed renewable for life regardless of future health conditions.
  • Higher monthly premium; requires a separate standalone Part D prescription plan.

Prescription Drug Formulation Review

Every California Medicare plan has a unique list of covered medications (formulary). One insurer might charge $10/month for your medicine while another charges $120. Send us your medication list—we check every tier to protect your wallet.
