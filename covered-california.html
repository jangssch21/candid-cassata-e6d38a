Understanding Covered California Plan Tiers

All plans cover the 10 Essential Health Benefits, including doctor visits, hospital care, prescriptions, and maternity care.

Bronze Tier

Lowest Premium

Plan pays ~60%, you pay ~40%. Best for healthy individuals seeking catastrophic protection and the absolute lowest monthly cost.

Ideal for: Low routine doctor visits
Highest Value
Silver 94

Enhanced Subsidies

Plan pays up to 94%. Qualifiers for Silver 94 enjoy $0/low deductibles and doctor copays as low as $5-$15.

Ideal for: Maximum cost savings
Gold Tier

Low Copays

Plan pays ~80%, you pay ~20%. Moderate-to-high premium with predictable, low copayments and no medical deductible.

Ideal for: Frequent specialist care
Platinum Tier

Zero Deductible

Plan pays ~90%, you pay ~10%. Highest monthly premium, but virtually all medical expenses are covered with minimal out-of-pocket fees.

Ideal for: Ongoing complex treatment

Need Coverage Outside Open Enrollment?

You may qualify to enroll today under a Special Enrollment Period (SEP) if you experienced any of the following life events within the past 60 days:

Lost job-based health insurance
Moved to/within California
Got married or entered domestic partnership
Had a baby or adopted a child
Our certified agents handle the paperwork and verification for your SEP submission.
